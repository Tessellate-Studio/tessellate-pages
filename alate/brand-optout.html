Privacy

Brand Opt-Out Request

If you operate a storefront and prefer Alate not to read your site, let us know. We will add your origin to our blocklist and stop requesting product pages from it.

How it works

  • Alate reads a public product page only for a user who has that page's link: when they paste or share it for a fit check, or — to show them the product's name and brand before they ask for one — when they have copied it and open the app's fit-check panel. We never crawl.
  • We identify ourselves with the User-Agent Alate/1.0 (+https://tessellate.co.in/alate/brand-optout.html).
  • We respect robots.txt: if your file disallows our path, we bail automatically.
  • We do not redistribute scraped data. Product information is shown only to the user who requested it, alongside a "View on Store" link back to your storefront.
  • If you want to opt out anyway — no justification required — send us the request below and we'll honour it within 24 hours.

Request removal

Email us with:

  • Your store's origin (e.g. yourstore.com)
  • A brief note if you'd like to tell us why (optional)
  • Your name and role (so we can confirm the request is from someone authorised)

Emailprivacy@tessellate.co.in

What happens nextWe add your origin to our blocklist within 24 hours. From then on we read nothing from your store — neither a fit check nor the name of a copied link. Any user who pastes a URL from your store will see a "This brand has asked not to be read — please visit their store directly" card instead of a fit analysis. Users can still reach you by tapping through to your storefront.
