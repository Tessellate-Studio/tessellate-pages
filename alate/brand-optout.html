Privacy

Brand Opt-Out Request

If you operate a storefront and prefer Alate not to read your site, let us know. We will add your origin to our blocklist and stop requesting product pages from it.

How it works

  • Alate reads a public product page only for a user who has that page's link: when they paste or share it for a fit check, or — to show them the product's name and brand before they ask for one — when they have copied it and open the app's fit-check panel. We never crawl.
  • Two follow-ups re-read a page a user already asked about, with nobody waiting on screen: if your store is too slow to read in time, we keep trying in the background for up to six hours; and if a user asked to hear when their size is back in stock, we re-check that one page about every six hours until it is back or the alert expires. Both follow the same opt-out and robots.txt rules as everything else.
  • If a user shares a link from your own app (an AppsFlyer OneLink or a similar link service), we ask the link service where it points and then read your product page as above. The opt-out and robots.txt rules apply to your store, not to the link service.
  • Every request carries the token Alate/1.0 (+https://tessellate.co.in/alate/brand-optout.html). It is added to the end of an ordinary browser User-Agent, because many stores turn away anything that doesn't look like a browser. The full string we send is:
    Mozilla/5.0 (Windows NT 10.0; Win64; x64) AppleWebKit/537.36 (KHTML, like Gecko) Chrome/120.0.0.0 Safari/537.36 Alate/1.0 (+https://tessellate.co.in/alate/brand-optout.html)
    When we unwrap an app link we send the same token on an Android phone's User-Agent instead.
  • We respect robots.txt. To address us, use User-agent: Alate. If your file has no group for us, we follow your * group. Because our full string contains Mozilla, Chrome and Safari, a group naming one of those applies to us too. If your file disallows the product's path, we stop before reading the page. If your robots.txt can't be fetched at all (for example, your server returns an error), we go ahead and read the page — the opt-out below doesn't depend on it.
  • We do not redistribute scraped data. Product information is shown only to the user who requested it, alongside a "View on Store" link back to your storefront.
  • If you want to opt out anyway — no justification required — send us the request below and we'll honour it within 24 hours.

Request removal

Email us with:

  • Your store's origin (e.g. yourstore.com)
  • A brief note if you'd like to tell us why (optional)
  • Your name and role (so we can confirm the request is from someone authorised)

Emailprivacy@tessellate.co.in

What happens nextWe add your origin to our blocklist within 24 hours. From then on we read nothing from your store — no fit check, no name for a copied link, no background retry and no restock re-check. Any user who pastes a URL from your store will see a card saying the brand has asked not to be scraped and to visit your store directly, instead of a fit analysis. Users can still reach you by tapping through to your storefront.
