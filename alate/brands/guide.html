What Alate for Brands does

Alate for Brands is a Shopify app for clothing brands. You add the sizing facts Shopify doesn't hold — size charts, fit offerings, model sizing and fabric. Alate then uses them to answer "what's my size?" in two places:

  • On your product pages, through the Alate Size Finder block.
  • In the Alate app, for shoppers who paste one of your product links.

Your catalogue, prices and stock stay in Shopify and are read live when needed; Alate keeps no copy of them.

Install

  1. Write to partners@tessellate.co.in with your store's URL. While Alate for Brands is in early access, we send you a private install link — it isn't in the public Shopify App Store yet.
  2. Open the link while signed in to your Shopify admin, review the permissions (listed below) and install.
  3. The app opens inside your Shopify admin with four pages: Home, Products, Size charts and Settings.

Home: three steps

Home shows a guide headed Set up in three steps. It hides once all three are done, and comes back if one slips.

  1. Add your size charts — opens Size charts.
  2. Tell Alate who you serve — opens Settings.
  3. Finish the products that need it — opens Products.

Below it, Show the size finder on your store checks your live theme and shows whether the block is on it — see Add the size finder.

Size charts

Size charts holds everything Alate uses to answer "will it fit me?" for your products. Each chart has a name, the sizes, a unit (cm or inches) and what it applies to: one product, a collection, or the whole shop.

Import instead of retyping

Tap Import size charts and choose where to look:

  • Scan my product descriptions — Alate looks through your product descriptions for size tables.
  • Paste a table — paste the HTML of a size-chart table, for example from your size-guide page.

Nothing is saved until you review it. Check each row, then save the ones you want.

Body measurements, not garment measurementsThe size finder on your product page answers from body measurements (chest, waist, hips). A product only shows the button once it has a chart with body measurements.

Applying a chart to a collection currently needs the collection's ID; a collection picker is on the way.

Who you serve and defaults

In Settings:

  • Who you serve — tick the shoppers you cut for: tall, petite, plus, wide, modest, slow fashion. It's stored with your shop and is how Alate matches you with shoppers whose body profile fits.
  • Defaults — the Measurement unit for new charts (centimetres or inches), and a shop default size chart that applies to any product without its own.

Products

Products shows how many of your products are ready for Alate shoppers, with search, each product's size chart, sizing data and status. You can also set fit offerings on many products at once. Open a product to edit it:

SectionWhat to add
Size chartUse the chart it inherits (from a collection or the shop default), add one for this product, or use a different chart.
Fit offeringsWhat you offer beyond the standard range — tall or petite sizes, longer or shorter legs, extra sleeve length or a shortened sleeve, an extended size range, made to measure. For each: extra length, price difference, lead time and how to order.
Model sizingModel height, Model wears size, and Notes (optional) — e.g. "Model sized up for a relaxed fit". Alate uses it to tell shoppers where the piece lands on them compared with the model.
FabricPaste from the care label to fill the fibre composition, and say how much it stretches: none, some or lots.
AvailabilityTick We won't restock this product and Alate stops offering back-in-stock alerts on its sold-out sizes.
Sizes past your rangeA count over the last 90 days of Alate shoppers outside your size range who showed interest in a bigger or smaller size. Interest, never requests — no names or measurements.

Add the size finder to your theme

On Home, the Show the size finder on your store card checks your live theme. Use its link to open the theme editor with the block ready to add, then press Save.

To add it by hand: in Online Store → Themes → Customize, open a product template, choose Add block → Apps → Alate Size Finder, place it near your size selector, then press Save.

  • The block has one setting, Button label (default "What's my size?").
  • It takes on your theme's fonts and colours.
  • It works on product pages only, and stays hidden on products it can't answer yet.

What shoppers see

A shopper taps the button and the panel opens: Answer with the size chart of this exact product. They can:

  • Enter your measurements — chest, waist and hips, in cm or inches, and height if they like — then Find my size. They get your size label, a fit note, and a flag if that size is sold out.
  • Use my Alate profile — connect with the Alate app (on Android today), approve on their phone, and get a size from their saved profile.

The panel promises: Sizes are calculated by Alate. Your measurements are used once and never stored or shared with this store. Your store receives only the size.

In the Alate app, shoppers who paste your product link get a fit card built on the same data, plus your fabric and model sizing.

Alate for Brands only reads. It never writes to your products, theme or orders, and it asks for no customer data. Details: privacy policy.

Uninstalling

Uninstall from Settings → Apps and sales channels in your Shopify admin. The block comes off your store with the app. Your store's connection and everything you entered — charts, offerings, product sizing, settings and the sizes-past-range counts — is deleted straight away, and Shopify's erase request 48 hours later clears anything left. Your catalogue, theme and checkout were never changed, so there's nothing to undo.
