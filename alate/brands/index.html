The cost of a guess

every fit return is margin that walked back.

On a small order, the chain can cost more than the margin.

  • Shipping both ways — usually on you
  • Inspection, repacking and restocking time
  • Stock in transit while the season moves on
  • Payment fees that don't come back with the refund
  • A customer whose first impression is "their sizing is off"

What your shoppers see

two places the answer shows up.

Both are built on the size data you add. Neither sends a shopper's body measurements to your store — only the size comes back.

01

on your product page

A "What's my size?" button — the Alate Size Finder block — appears on any product you've given a body-measurement size chart. It stays hidden on products it can't answer.

No app needed
02

enter a few measurements

Chest, waist and hips, plus height if they like, in cm or inches. They get your size label back, a note on the fit, and a heads-up if that size is sold out — "find your size for when it's back".

Find my size
03

or use their alate profile

Alate shoppers tap "Use my Alate profile", approve on their phone, and get the size from the profile they already have.

Connect with Alate
04

in the alate app

Shoppers who paste your product link into Alate get a fit card built on your data: which of your sizes to order, where it sits on them, your fabric, your model's height against theirs, and whether their size is in stock.

a promise on the button

Setup — an afternoon, no developer

set up in three steps.

The app's Home page walks you through them and hides itself when you're done.

00

install from your link

We send you a private install link. It connects to your store; your theme code, checkout and catalogue stay as they are.

01

add your size charts

Paste a table, or let Alate scan your product descriptions for size charts. You review every row before it's saved. One chart can cover a product, a collection or the whole shop.

02

tell alate who you serve

Tick the shoppers you cut for — tall, petite, plus, wide, modest, slow fashion. It's stored with your shop, and it's how Alate matches you with shoppers whose body profile fits.

03

finish the products that need it

Products shows how many are "ready for Alate shoppers". Open one to add its chart, fit offerings, model sizing and fabric.

✓

show the size finder

Home checks your live theme and gives you a one-tap link into the theme editor that adds the block. Press Save, and it's live.

What you control

every size a shopper sees comes from you.

size charts

Body measurements per product, per collection, or one chart for the whole shop — in cm or inches.

fit offerings

Tall and petite ranges, longer or shorter legs, extra or shorter sleeves, extended sizes, made to measure — each with the extra length, price difference, lead time and how to order.

model sizing

Your model's height and the size they wear, per product — so Alate can say where a hem lands on someone who isn't the model's height.

fabric

Paste the care label for the fibre composition, and say how much it stretches: none, some or lots.

restock alerts

Tick "We won't restock this product" and Alate stops offering back-in-stock alerts on it, so nobody signs up to wait.

sizes past your range

A 90-day count, per product, of Alate shoppers who fell outside your sizes and showed interest in a bigger or smaller one. Interest, never requests — no names, no measurements.

defaults

The measurement unit for new charts, and a default size chart for the whole shop.

Your data

your catalogue stays in shopify.

Alate only adds what Shopify doesn't hold. Here's what it reads, keeps and never touches.

What it reads, live

  • Products, prices, options, stock, tags and descriptions — when a shopper asks, or when you import charts
  • Your live theme — only to check the block is on it

What it keeps

  • What you type in: size charts, fit offerings, model sizing, fabric, who you serve, defaults
  • Your store's connection, with its access token encrypted

From shoppers

  • Measurements typed into the block — used once to work out the size, never stored, never sent to your store
  • An "Alate profile" hand-off — held ten minutes at most
  • Sizes-past-range taps — a count with no name, deleted after 90 days

When you uninstall

  • The block comes off your store with the app
  • Your store's connection and everything you entered is deleted straight away
  • Shopify's 48-hour erase request clears anything left

Where it lives

  • Supabase (Singapore) for what Alate keeps
  • Vercel for the app and its API
  • Sentry for error reports, with headers, cookies and queries scrubbed

Never

  • No copy of your catalogue, prices or stock
  • No Shopify customer data
  • No changes to your theme code, checkout or products
  • No cookies from the block

Questions brands ask

the short answers.

The full walkthrough is in the merchant guide.

Do I need a developer?

No. You install from a link we send you, add your charts in the app, and switch the "Alate Size Finder" block on in your theme editor. Nothing in your theme code changes.

What if my sizing is unusual?

That's the point. Alate matches a shopper to your chart, not a generic S/M/L — so a brand that runs small, or cuts for tall or petite bodies, gets answered on its own terms.

What does a shopper have to share?

On your store: a few measurements they type in themselves, used once and discarded — or a hand-off from their Alate profile. Your store only ever receives the size.

Does the block slow my store down?

It's a small theme app block that loads only on product pages and stays hidden where it can't answer. We're measuring its effect on page speed on a test store before the public listing.

What does it cost?

Early-access brands are set up by hand. Write to us and we'll talk it through.

What happens if I uninstall?

The block disappears and what you entered is deleted. Your catalogue, theme and checkout were never modified, so there's nothing to undo.
