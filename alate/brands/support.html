Setup, the size finder block, your data — and a person to write to.

Email uspartners@tessellate.co.in — include your store's .myshopify.com address. We aim to reply within two business days. Privacy requests: privacy@tessellate.co.in.

Step-by-step setup is in the merchant guide.

The size finder button doesn't show

  • Is the block on your live theme? The app's Home page checks for you. If it says it's missing, use its link to add it, or add Add block → Apps → Alate Size Finder to your product template, and press Save.
  • Does the product have a body-measurement size chart? The button only appears on products Alate can answer. Open the product in Products and check its Size chart — its own, one from a collection, or the shop default.
  • Is it a product page? The block works on product templates only.

The size seems wrong

  • Check the chart the product uses — it may be inheriting a collection or shop-default chart meant for a different cut.
  • Check the chart's unit (cm or inches) matches the numbers in it.
  • Still off? Email us the product link and the measurements you tried.

Common questions

Is it in the Shopify App Store?

Not yet. During early access we set brands up by hand with a private install link.

Does it change my theme, products or checkout?

No. It only reads. The size finder is a theme app block you add and remove in the theme editor; nothing in your theme code changes.

Do shoppers' measurements reach my store?

No. Measurements typed into the block go to Alate to work out the size, are used once and are not stored. Your store receives only the size.

Can I import my existing size guide?

Yes — in Size charts → Import size charts, scan your product descriptions or paste a size-chart table. You review every row before anything is saved.

What are "sizes past your range"?

A 90-day count, per product, of Alate shoppers who fell outside your size range and showed interest in a bigger or smaller size. It carries no names or measurements.

How do I stop back-in-stock alerts for a product?

Open it in Products and tick We won't restock this product under Availability.

What happens to my data if I uninstall?

Everything you entered is deleted straight away, and Shopify's erase request 48 hours later clears anything left. See the privacy policy.

I don't use the app — can I stop Alate reading my product pages?

Yes. Shoppers using the Alate app can paste any public product link; if you'd rather Alate didn't read your store, see brand opt-out.
