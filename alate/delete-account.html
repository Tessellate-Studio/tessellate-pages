Privacy Support

Delete Your Data

When you're not signed in, everything Alate knows about you lives on your device — delete it from within the app. If you signed in with Google (optional), a synced copy lives in your account, and there's a separate path to delete that.

What's stored where

  • Body profile (height, proportions, exact measurements) — on your device. If you're signed in, also in your account.
  • Fit check history — on your device. If you're signed in, your most recent fit checks (up to 50) are also in your account.
  • Fit-calibration garments and budget range — on your device. If you're signed in, also in your account.
  • Age-gate confirmation — on your device only.
  • Device identifier (random UUID for rate-limiting) — on your device; the server sees it but doesn't link it to you.
  • Google account info (email + name, only if you signed in) — stored via Supabase Auth server-side.
  • Brand-request notify email (only if you opted in to "notify me when added" on an unsupported-brand fit check) — stored server-side until the notification is sent, then deleted within 30 days — or deleted 90 days after you asked, if we haven't been able to send it.
  • Restock-alert entries (email, product URL, size — only if you asked for one) — your email and any identifier are removed once the email is sent, the alert expires, or the moment you unsubscribe; the brand, product link and size stay only as demand data.
  • Shared measurement snapshots (only if you shared a link) — deleted when the link expires, or earlier on request.

Delete from the app

Body profile

  1. Open Alate and tap the Profile tab
  2. Scroll to the bottom
  3. Tap Delete my body profile and confirm

Fit history

  1. Open Alate and tap the History tab
  2. Tap Clear history at the bottom of the screen

Everything else on the device (including age-gate and device ID): uninstall the app. OS-level uninstall removes all device-local storage.

If you're signed in, deleting your profile or history in the app syncs that deletion to your account too. Signing out clears the synced copy from that device but leaves it in your account, so a later sign-in restores it — to remove the account copy for good, use the next section.

Delete your signed-in account

If you opted in to Google Sign-In, deleting the account removes the Supabase Auth record and every synced row — profile, measurements, budget, calibration and fit history. To request deletion:

Emailprivacy@tessellate.co.in

Please include the email address associated with your Google sign-in. We will confirm deletion within 30 days.

Delete a brand-request notify email

If you opted in to be notified when an unsupported brand is added (via the "notify me" prompt on the fit-check error screen), you can request deletion of that record without deleting anything else.

Emailprivacy@tessellate.co.in

Use the subject "Delete brand-request notify - Alate" and include the email address you provided. We will remove the entry within 30 days.

Deletion is permanentWhen you're not signed in, your body profile and fit history are stored only on your device — uninstalling the app deletes them. A fit check sends your profile to our server to work out your size, and it is discarded straight after; nothing is kept.
