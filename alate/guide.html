Getting started

Alate is in early access on Android (Google Play testing) and iPhone (TestFlight). Write to us and we'll send you an invite link.

  1. Open Alate. The first screens explain what it does and ask nothing of you.
  2. Confirm your age — you need to be 16 or older (18 or older in India).
  3. Read how your data is handled and tap I agree. You can also choose whether to share usage statistics; it's off unless you switch it on.
  4. That's it. No account is needed. Your body questions come at your first fit check.

Your body profile

The first time you check a fit, Alate asks eleven quick questions, each answered with a tap:

  • Which sizes you shop — women's, men's or unisex
  • Your height
  • Your shoulders, arm length, bust, torso, leg length, waist, tummy, hips and thighs — each by shape or length, not by number

Your answers are saved on your phone. To change one later, go to Profile. When you change your answers, your older fit checks re-check themselves the next time you open the app, newest first.

Know your exact measurements?Entering numbers, scanning a tailor's sheet and sharing measurements are Advanced features, available on request — see Advanced features.

Checking a fit

Find a piece of clothing in any online store, then bring the link to Alate in whichever way is easiest:

  • Share it. In the store's app or your browser, tap Share and pick Alate.
  • Copy it, then open Alate. When you open the fit-check panel, Alate spots the copied link and shows the product's name and brand before you ask for a check.
  • Paste it into the fit-check panel.

Links from a brand's own app (for example a "share" link that opens the brand's app) are followed through to the product page. Links to an app-store page aren't products, so Alate says so instead of checking them.

Alate sizes clothing. Shoes aren't sized yet.

Reading the fit card

Every fit check becomes a card. From top to bottom, it can show:

Part of the cardWhat it tells you
Size to tryThe size likely to fit you, in the brand's own sizes — EU 38 or IT 42 if that's how the brand sizes, S/M/L if that's how it sizes.
ConfidenceHow sure Alate is, and why: the brand's own size chart, a garment measurement, or a general standard chart. A size from a standard chart is never shown as sure, and the card says so.
Works for youWhere the piece suits your body, one idea per line.
Watch-outsWhere it may sit tight, loose, short or long on you. "Nothing to flag" means just that.
The model and youWhen the store lists the model's height and size, how they compare with you — for example where a hem lands on you versus on the model.
FabricLined or not, knit or woven, bias cut, how much it stretches and recovers, how it drapes.
See-throughHow sheer it's likely to be, from buyers' answers and a read of the store's product photo.
What buyers sayNotes like "7 of 8 buyers say the sleeves run long" — only when at least five people answered and most agree.
StockWhether your size is in stock right now.
Budget chip$, $$ or $$$ against the budget you set, converted to your currency.
Outside the rangeWhen the brand doesn't make your size. You can tap to say you'd have bought a bigger or smaller size — the brand sees a count, never who you are.

No AI writes your card. The words come from fixed rules applied to the brand's data and your answers. AI is only used to read product pages and product photos (colours, how sheer), and — if you use it — a tailor's sheet.

Tap View on store to go to the product and buy it there. Alate never handles your order or payment.

Sold out, slow stores, missing brands

Your size is sold out

Tap Notify on the card. Alate re-checks that product about every six hours and tells you in the app when your size is back — and by email too if you're signed in. If the brand has said it won't restock a piece, the option doesn't appear. A fit check that has been sold out for 45 days and isn't coming back can be deleted for good.

The store is slow

Some stores take longer to read than you'd want to wait. Alate says it's taking longer, keeps trying in the background for up to six hours, and puts a note in your notifications when the card is ready — or if it couldn't be read.

The brand isn't supported yet

Tap Notify me when added. We'll tell you in the app (and by one email if you're signed in) when it's supported.

The store asked not to be read, or blocks us

Some stores have asked Alate not to read their pages, and some block automated reading. For those, Alate stops and offers you a link straight to the store instead of a fit card.

Home: your fit checks

  • Every fit check is a card in the deck on Home, the good fits up front.
  • Tap a card to open the full analysis; swipe to move between them.
  • Swipe a card up, off the deck, to delete it. Tap Undo on the note if you change your mind.

Bought it, and how it fit

  1. On the card, tap Bought it and choose the size you ordered — Alate's pick is pre-selected.
  2. When it's arrived, the card asks How did it fit? Say whether it was too small, just right or too large, which size you got, where it was off, how it sits — and, if you like, whether it was see-through and how the length or sleeves were.
  3. Your bought pieces live on your Pieces page and follow your account if you're signed in. A note you type stays on your phone.

Answers that say a piece was too small or too large, and see-through, length and sleeve answers, reach us without your name or account. They're what make "what buyers say" possible for the next shopper.

Budget

Set a budget range once in Profile. Every card then shows a $, $$ or $$$ chip, converted to your currency, so you know where a piece sits before you fall for it.

Signing in and syncing

Signing in is optional, and uses your Google account. When you're signed in, these follow you to any phone you sign in on: your body profile and any exact measurements, your budget, your fit-calibration garments, your last 50 fit checks and up to 100 bought pieces.

Signing out clears that copy from the phone but keeps it in your account, so signing in again brings it back. To remove it for good, delete the account (below).

Advanced features

Advanced features are switched on by request, for one email address, and work while you're signed in with that Google account — on every phone.

Asking for them

Go to Profile → Measurements and tap Enter your measurements, and send the request. When we've switched it on, a note appears in your notifications.

What they are

  • Your own numbers — up to nine measurements in cm or inches, inside leg included. Your fit checks then use them.
  • Scan a tailor's sheet — photograph a measurement sheet, crop it to just the numbers (leave out any name or phone number), check what Alate read, then save. The photo is read once and not kept.
  • Share measurements by link — choose which measurements to share and for how long (one week to three months). Your Shared links list shows each live link, how many times it was opened, and how long it has left; tap Stop sharing to end one at once. The person you send it to sees a simple web page; no account needed.

Notifications

Alate keeps your notifications inside the app: a size back in stock, a brand you asked for now supported, a slow fit check that's ready, and Advanced features switched on. Restock and brand notices are also emailed if you're signed in.

Your data and deleting it

Your body profile and fit checks live on your phone. Each fit check sends your profile to our server to work out your size; it's used for that check and thrown away. The one-page summary lists what Alate needs, reads and keeps; the privacy policy has every detail.

  • Delete one fit check: swipe its card up on Home.
  • Delete your profile (and your saved and bought pieces): Profile → Delete my profile.
  • Delete your account and everything synced to it: Profile → Delete account.
  • Usage statistics: switch Share usage stats to help improve Alate off in Profile to stop them at once.
  • Everything on the phone: uninstall the app.

No longer have the app? See delete your data.

When something doesn't work

  • The card says the store can't be read yet — tap Notify me when added; we prioritise the stores people ask for.
  • The size seems off — check your answers in Profile, and look at the card's confidence reason: a size from a general standard chart is a starting point, not the brand's own word.
  • Advanced features aren't showing — make sure you're signed in with the same Google email you asked with.
  • Something else — email connect@tessellate.co.in with your phone model and, if you can, the product link. See support.
