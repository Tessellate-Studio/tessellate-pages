Buying blind

a medium here is a large over there.

Every brand cuts differently, and you usually find out when the parcel arrives.

  • Ordering two sizes to be safe, and waiting for the refund on the one that goes back
  • Repacking the box and finding time to drop it off
  • Keeping things that almost fit because sending them back felt like too much work
  • Only buying from the couple of brands whose sizing you already know

How it works

answer a few questions once, then check any link.

01

tell alate about your body

There are eleven questions about which sizes you shop, your height, and the shape of your shoulders, arms, bust, torso, legs, waist, tummy, hips and thighs. You answer by tapping, so you won't need a tape measure or an account.

you'll see them at your first fit check

02

bring a link

Share a product from the store's app or website, or paste the link. You can also just copy it and open Alate, which will notice the link and show you the product's name. Share links from a brand's own app work too.

handy when you find something at midnight

03

read the fit card

You get the size that's likely to fit, in that brand's sizes, along with how sure Alate is and why. Below that it goes through each part of your body, then covers the fabric, stock and price.

all before you've paid for anything

04

if the store is slow

Some shops take a while to load. Alate keeps trying in the background for up to six hours and lets you know in the app once your fit check is ready.

you can close the app meanwhile

The fit card

what's on a fit card.

Each line on the card comes from somewhere you can check, like the brand's size chart, the product page or what other buyers told us. The card says where.

the brand's own sizes

If the brand sizes in numbers (EU 38, IT 42, UK 12) you'll see a number, and if it uses letters you'll see a letter. You order exactly what the card says.

Size to try

how sure, and why

Each size comes with a word for how confident Alate is and the reason behind it. When the brand has no chart of its own and Alate falls back on a general one, the card says so and never calls that size sure.

Confidence

what works and what to watch

Where it sits close, where there's room, and anything to look out for. Each point gets its own line.

Works for you

the model and you

If the store lists the model's height and size, Alate compares them with yours. A hem that sits at the model's knee might land mid-calf on you, and the card will tell you.

Model vs you

what the fabric will do

Alate reads the product page to find out whether it's lined, knitted or woven, cut on the bias, and how much it stretches and drapes.

Fabric build

see-through?

How sheer it's likely to be, based on what buyers told us and a look at the store's product photo.

what buyers say

Lines like "7 of 8 buyers say the sleeves run long". We only show these once at least five people have answered and most of them agree.

Home

every fit you've checked, in one deck.

The best fits sit at the front, and the coat you looked at last month is still there on payday.

a deck of cards

Each fit check is a card. Tap one to open the full details and swipe sideways to move between them. To delete one, swipe it up (there's an Undo if you change your mind).

it keeps itself up to date

If you change your answers, your older cards check themselves again the next time you open the app, starting with the newest.

bought it

When you buy something, mark it as bought and pick the size you ordered. Alate's suggestion is already selected. If you're signed in, your pieces move with your account.

how did it fit?

Once it arrives, tell Alate whether it was too small, just right or too big, and where. We get those answers without your name, and they're what the "what buyers say" lines are built from.

sign in only if you want to

You can sign in with Google if you want your profile, budget, last 50 fit checks and bought pieces on your other phones. If you don't, everything stays on this one.

Advanced, on request

if you know your measurements.

You can ask for these in the app under Profile → Measurements. Once we've turned them on for your email address, they work on any phone signed in with that Google account.

  • Enter up to nine of your own measurements in cm or inches, including inside leg, and every fit check will use them.
  • Take a photo of a tailor's measurement sheet. Alate reads the numbers and you check them before saving.
  • Share your measurements with someone by link. You choose which ones to include and how long the link lasts, anywhere from a week to three months. You can see how often it's been opened and stop it whenever you like.

Your data

what alate knows about you.

Your body profile is kept on your phone. This is what else Alate uses, and where it goes.

What it needs

  • Your answers to the eleven questions
  • A product link
  • You don't need an account or to give your name

What it reads

  • The one product page you gave it: name, sizes, size chart, price, stock, fabric, model details
  • The store's product photo, for colour and see-through
  • Your clipboard only when you open the fit-check panel, and only to spot a link

On your phone

  • Body profile and budget
  • Fit checks and bought pieces
  • Your shared-links list
  • A random ID made when you install

Sent, then discarded

  • Your profile, which goes with each fit check and is deleted once the check is done
  • The product link, which is deleted after the page is read

Kept only when you ask

  • If you sign in, a copy of your data until you delete the account
  • Restock alerts, with your email removed once the alert has gone out
  • Shared links, until they expire or you stop them
  • The link to a slow store, for six hours at most
  • Usage stats if you turn them on. These use a random ID and never include your name, measurements or links

Never

  • Ads, or selling your data
  • Tracking you across other apps
  • Photos of you
  • AI writing your fit card. The card comes from fixed rules, and AI is only used to read product pages and photos

For the details, read the privacy policy. You can also delete your data, and stores can read how Alate reads a page.

Questions

questions people ask.

More in the guide and on support.

Which stores does it work with?

Lots of online clothing stores, though not all of them yet. If a store isn't supported, tap "Notify me when added" on the card and we'll tell you when it is. A few stores have asked us not to read their pages or block automated reading, and for those Alate just sends you to the store.

Is it a guarantee?

No. It's the size most likely to fit you, and the card tells you how sure Alate is and why. A size worked out from the brand's own chart is more reliable than one from a general chart, and you'll see which was used.

Why does it say "38" and not "M"?

Because that's how the brand labels its sizes. Alate uses the brand's own sizes so you can order exactly what the card says.

Do I need an account?

No, fit checks work without one. Signing in with Google lets you use your profile on more than one phone, and you'll need it for Advanced features.

Shoes?

Not yet. For now Alate only sizes clothing.

Where can I get it?

Alate is in early access on Android through Google Play testing and on iPhone through TestFlight. and we'll send you an invite.
