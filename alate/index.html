What buying blind costs you

Sizing isn't standard — a medium in one brand is a large in another. The label was never the problem. The guessing is.

  • Ordering two sizes because you can't tell — and fronting the money for both
  • The repackaging, the drop-off trip, the refund that takes its time
  • A wardrobe of almost-fits you keep because returning felt like work
  • Sticking to the same two brands because at least you know their sizing

What Alate changes

One verdict, built on your body — not the model's.

A verdict for your body

Paste or share a product link. Alate reads the garment and tells you where it lands on your proportions — snug here, loose there — not how it drapes on a sample-size model.

Trust new brands on the first order

Brands you've been eyeing stop being a gamble. When the fit verdict is built on your measurements — and often on the brand's own size data — the first order can be as safe as the tenth.
