Privacy Policy

Version 7 · Effective September 19, 2026

Alate ("we", "our", "the app") is a mobile application developed by Tessellate Studio that helps you predict whether clothing items will fit based on your body profile. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

TL;DRYour body profile lives on your device. When you run a fit check it is sent to our own server to compute the result, then immediately discarded — never shared with anyone, and your stored profile is never sent to an AI service. The one exception is a feature you have to go and use: if you scan a tailor's measurement sheet, that photo goes once to our AI provider to read the numbers off it, and is not stored (section 1i). If you choose to sign in, a copy of your profile and fit history is kept in your account so your other devices can pull it, and is deleted when you delete the account. Product URLs you paste are read in real time and discarded — unless a store is too slow to read while you wait, when we hold that one link just long enough to finish the job and hand the result to your app (six hours at the very most). We don't sell data, don't run ads, don't track you across apps.

1. Data We Collect

a) Body Profile Data

When you build your profile, you provide height and six body proportions: shoulder, chest, waist, hip, thigh type, torso length — and, optionally, exact measurements in cm or inches. This data is stored on your device via AsyncStorage. When you run a fit check, your body profile is sent to our own backend so our fit algorithm can compute the prediction. It is used in memory for that single request and then discarded — it is not logged, not used to build any profile of you, and never sent to any third party or AI service. If you sign in (optional), a copy of your profile is also stored in your account so it can be restored on your other devices — see section 1e.

One clarification, because the sentence above is absolute and one optional feature sits outside it: the profile we hold is never sent to an AI service. If you choose to scan a tailor's measurement sheet, you are handing us a photo of a measurement sheet — which you can crop first to leave out the customer's name and number — and that image is read once by our AI provider — see section 1i for exactly what happens to it. Your stored profile is not part of that request.

b) Product URLs (ephemeral, with one short-lived exception)

When you paste or share a product URL, we send that URL to our backend to read publicly available product information (name, price, image, description, material, sizes) from the source website. The URL is processed in real time and discarded, and we do not persist URLs with your name, email or account. One exception: some stores are too slow to read while you wait. When that happens we save that link, together with your device's random app identifier, so we can keep trying in the background and hand the result to your app the next time it asks. That entry is deleted the moment your app confirms it has the result, within 15 minutes of delivery if it never confirms, and in every case within six hours. It is used for nothing else. The fit check result is stored locally on your device as part of your fit history.

c) Fit Check History

Results of your fit checks (product name, image URL, fit score, warnings, recommended size, timestamps) are stored locally on your device. If you sign in, your most recent fit checks (up to 50) are also stored in your account for cross-device sync — see section 1e.

d) Device Identifier

On first launch, the app generates a random UUID stored locally and includes it as an X-Device-Id header on backend requests. It is not an advertising ID and is never tied to your body profile. It is used for rate-limiting (to prevent abuse) and to route things you asked for back to this install: a slow store read finishing in the background (section 1b), a restock alert and its push notification (sections 1j and 1k), and — in a scrambled, per-product form — a size-range tap (section 1l). On its own it carries no name, email or account, but where you give us those for a feature it is stored in the same record: a restock alert keeps it next to your email, and the push-token record keeps it next to your account id if you are signed in. That is why section 13 treats it as linked to you. It is regenerated if you reinstall the app.

Our crash-reporting tools generate a second, separate per-install identifier of their own, described in section 1f. That one is never stored with your name, email or account, and neither identifier is an advertising ID.

e) Google Account and Cloud Sync (optional)

If you choose to sign in with Google on the Account screen, we receive your email address and profile name via Supabase Auth. Sign-in is optional. Your body profile, fit history, and calibration work fully without an account.

Signing in turns on cloud sync. The following are stored in your account in our database (Supabase) and pulled to any device you sign in on: your body profile, exact measurements if you entered them, your budget range, your fit-calibration garments, and your most recent fit checks (up to 50). Signing out clears them from that device; the account copy stays until you delete the account (section 7). Synced data is protected by row-level security so only your signed-in session can read or write it, and it is never used for any purpose other than restoring your own profile.

f) Crash Reports

We use Sentry and Firebase Crashlytics to collect crash reports and performance traces. These include stack traces, device model, OS version, app version, and a random identifier that stays the same for as long as the app is installed. That identifier is what lets us tell one person hitting a bug fifty times from fifty people hitting it once — it is not tied to your name, email, account or body profile, and it is regenerated if you reinstall.

No body profile data, account details, or the addresses of products you look at are included in crash reports. Where a crash report needs to name a website, it records only the site (for example assets.example.com) and never the full address, because the rest of the address would reveal the specific item you were viewing.

We do not collect your location. Crash reports previously carried an approximate country inferred from your IP address; as of September 2026 we strip that before the report is sent and instruct our provider not to store IP addresses. Crash data helps us fix bugs and is retained for 90 days.

g) Brand Request Data (server-side)

If you paste a URL from a brand we don't yet support, the URL and brand domain are recorded in our backend so we can prioritise which storefronts to onboard next. We do not contact the brand on your behalf, and your body profile is never associated with the request.

Optionally, you may opt in to be notified by email when we add support for that brand. If you provide an email, it is stored alongside the brand entry for the sole purpose of sending that single notification — never used for marketing, newsletters, or any other communication. We keep it until that notification is sent, then remove it within 30 days; if we haven't been able to send it within 90 days of your request, we delete it then (section 6). We do not store your account id with brand requests. Providing this email is optional; the rest of the app works without it. Asking to be notified without giving an email works too: the app remembers the brand on your device and tells you inside the app. To know when, it occasionally asks our server whether the product you pasted can be checked yet — that request carries only the product link, which is read and discarded like any other (section 1b).

h) Shared Measurement Snapshots (optional, server-side)

If you choose to share your measurements, the specific values you select are stored anonymously on our servers — with no name, account, or device identifier attached — behind a random link. You pick how long the link lives (from 1 week to 3 months); the snapshot is automatically deleted once it expires. Sharing is entirely optional, and only the fields you tick are included.

i) Measurement Sheet Scanning (optional, not stored)

If you choose to scan a tailor's measurement sheet, the photo is sent once to our AI provider — Google (Gemini), or OpenAI if Google is unavailable — solely to read the numbers on it. Before the photo is sent, the app lets you crop it so that names, phone numbers or anything else you don't want read are left out, and we instruct the AI to ignore any personal details that remain. Under both providers' paid API terms, that image is not used to train any AI model, and we do not store the photo. The provider may keep a copy for a limited time to detect abuse of its service, then deletes it — OpenAI states up to 30 days; Google says a limited period without naming one. Only the values you review and approve are saved, and they stay on your device (and in your account, if you are signed in).

j) Restock Alerts (optional, email waitlist)

If your recommended size is sold out, you may ask to be emailed when it returns. If you opt in, we store your email address, the product URL, and the size — solely to send that back-in-stock notification. Stock is re-checked against the live store; we never store the merchant's inventory. The email is never used for marketing, and you can unsubscribe from any alert email.

The product URL is kept for three reasons and no others: it is the page we re-check to see whether your size has come back, it is the link in the email that takes you to the store, and it is what opens the product page when you tap the push notification. Once the notification is sent, you unsubscribe, or the alert expires, we remove your email address and any account or device identifier from the alert at that moment. The brand, product link and size remain only as an anonymous count of demand for that size, which cannot be traced back to you.

k) Push Notification Token (optional)

If you allow notifications, your device is issued a push token by Expo’s push service, and we store it so a restock alert can reach you on the device that asked for it. The row holds the token, the random device identifier from section 1d, your account id if you are signed in, and whether the device is iOS or Android — nothing else. It is used only to deliver alerts you asked for, never for marketing, and it is revoked when you turn notifications off. Email remains the guaranteed channel; push is an addition to it, not a replacement.

l) Size-Range Interest (optional)

When a brand doesn't make a size that fits you and we can tell which end of its range you fall off, the fit result offers a tap to show the brand you'd buy a bigger (or smaller) size. If you tap it, we record only the store, the product, and whether you wanted bigger or smaller — no size, no measurement, no name, email or account. To count each person once, we also store a one-way scrambled code made from your random app identifier (section 1d) together with that store and product. The code can't be turned back into the identifier, and taps on two different products produce unrelated codes, so they can't be linked into a history. The brand sees only a count, through Alate for Brands, our tool for stores. Entries are deleted after 90 days.

2. Age Requirement

Alate is intended for users aged 16 or older. On first launch, the app asks you to confirm your age. If you indicate you're under 16, the app will not collect body profile data from you. Users under 16 who circumvent the age gate by providing inaccurate information are not our intended audience; we will promptly delete any data we discover was collected from someone under 16.

3. How We Read Product Data

Alate fetches publicly accessible product pages to extract fit-relevant information. Our practice:

  • We identify ourselves with the User-Agent Alate/1.0 (+https://tessellate.co.in/alate/brand-optout.html) so site owners can identify and contact us.
  • We respect robots.txt — if an origin's robots.txt disallows automated access to a product path, we bail with a "please visit the store directly" message.
  • We respect explicit opt-outs — any brand can request removal via our opt-out page. Listed brands are skipped on subsequent fetches.
  • We do not bulk-crawl — we only fetch URLs explicitly submitted by a user.
  • We do not redistribute catalogues — product data is used to generate a fit verdict for the user who submitted the URL; it is not aggregated, resold, or served as a browseable catalogue.
  • We point users back to the merchant — the fit card links directly to the source store via a "View on Store" button and a "Data from {brand}" attribution line.

5. Security

  • All network communication uses HTTPS / TLS.
  • Local data (body profile, fit history, age confirmation, device ID) is stored in AsyncStorage, which is sandboxed per-app by the OS.
  • Authentication tokens (if you sign in) are stored in Expo SecureStore, backed by iOS Keychain / Android KeyStore.
  • Synced account data is protected by database row-level security: every read and write runs under your own signed-in session, and no other user can reach it.
  • Our backend enforces rate limits and input validation. URLs are validated before fetching to prevent SSRF.

6. Data Retention

  • Local data persists until you clear it or uninstall the app.
  • Synced account data (body profile, measurements, budget range, calibration, fit history) is retained until you delete your account, then permanently deleted within 30 days.
  • Crash reports are retained for 90 days.
  • Rate-limit counters reset every minute and are not persisted.
  • Brand opt-out entries are retained indefinitely so the opt-out persists across deployments.
  • Brand request entries (URL + brand domain) are retained indefinitely as anonymised demand signal. Optional notify-me email addresses are deleted within 30 days of the notification being sent, or upon your request — whichever is sooner. Until the notification is sent, the email is kept so that we can send it — but never for more than 90 days after your request. If we haven't been able to send it by then, the email is deleted and the brand entry stays as anonymous demand signal.
  • Product URLs from successful reads are never retained. The one exception is a store too slow to read while you wait: that link is kept — with your device's random app identifier, never your name, email or account — until your app collects the result. It is deleted on confirmation, within 15 minutes of delivery if your app never confirms, and never kept beyond six hours.
  • Shared measurement snapshots are deleted automatically when the link you chose expires (1 week to 3 months), and can be revoked earlier on request.
  • Measurement-sheet photos are never stored — the image is read once and discarded.
  • Restock-alert entries — your email address and any account or device identifier are removed as soon as the notification is sent, you unsubscribe, or the alert expires. The brand, product URL and size are kept afterwards only as anonymous demand data.
  • Size-range taps (store, product, bigger-or-smaller and a scrambled code) are deleted after 90 days.
  • Push notification tokens are kept while notifications are enabled and revoked when you turn them off or uninstall the app.

7. Your Rights

At any time you can:

  • Delete your body profile from Account → Delete my body profile.
  • Clear your fit history from the History screen → Clear history.
  • Sign out (if signed in) from Account → Sign out. This clears the synced copy from that device; the account copy remains until you delete the account.
  • Request full account deletion via our web deletion page. All server-side account data — including every synced profile, measurement and history row — is permanently deleted within 30 days.
  • Delete a brand-request notify email by emailing privacy@tessellate.co.in with the subject "Delete brand-request notify - Alate" and the email address you provided.
  • Request a copy of your data by emailing us at the address below.

8. Brand Opt-Out

If you operate a storefront and prefer Alate not read your site, submit an opt-out request at this page. Your origin will be added to our blocklist within 24 hours. We honour the opt-out even if it conflicts with our technical ability to read the site; we do not require justification.

9. Additional Rights for EEA/UK Residents (GDPR)

If you are in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, you have additional rights under the General Data Protection Regulation:

  • Legal basis: We process body profile data based on your explicit consent (via the age gate and profile setup), and sync it to your account based on your consent when you choose to sign in. We process product URLs based on legitimate interest (delivering the fit prediction you requested). We process crash data based on legitimate interest (app stability).
  • Right of access, rectification, erasure, portability, objection, withdrawal of consent — all exercisable by emailing privacy@tessellate.co.in. We respond within 30 days.
  • Cross-border transfers: Our backend runs on Vercel and Supabase infrastructure, which may route data through regions outside the EU/UK. Standard contractual clauses apply via our providers' DPAs.
  • Automated decision-making: Fit predictions are generated algorithmically but are not legally significant. You can always disregard the recommendation.
