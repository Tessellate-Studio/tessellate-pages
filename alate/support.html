Common questions

How does a fit check work?

Answer eleven quick questions about your body, then give Alate a clothing product link — share it from the store's app or website, paste it, or copy it and open the fit-check panel. Alate returns the size likely to fit you, in that brand's own sizes, with notes on where it will sit on you. Reading the fit card.

Why is my size a number like 38?

Because that's how the brand sizes it. Alate answers in the brand's own sizes so you order exactly what the card says.

How sure is the size?

The card shows a confidence word and its reason. A size from the brand's own chart is surer than one from a general standard chart — and a standard-chart size is never shown as sure.

Can I enter my exact measurements?

Entering your own numbers, scanning a tailor's measurement sheet and sharing your measurements are Advanced features. Ask for them in Profile → Measurements, under "Enter your measurements". Once approved, they work on every phone signed in to that Google account.

A brand or link didn't work.

Some stores can't be read yet — tap "Notify me when added" and we'll tell you when it is. Slow stores are retried in the background for up to six hours. A few stores have asked not to be read, or block automated reading; for those, Alate sends you straight to the store instead.

My size is sold out.

Tap "Notify" on the fit card. Alate tells you in the app when that size is back — and, if you're signed in, by email too.

How do I stop a shared measurement link?

Open your Shared links list in the app and tap "Stop sharing" on the link. It stops at once. Deleting your account stops every live link.

Does Alate work for shoes?

Not yet — Alate sizes clothing today.

Do I need an account?

No. Fit checks work with no sign-in. Google sign-in is optional; if you use it, your profile, budget, fit checks and the pieces you've marked as bought sync across your phones.

Where is my data?

Your body profile and fit checks live on your phone. If you sign in, a copy is kept in your account so your other phones can pull it. Details in the privacy policy.

How do I delete my account?

In the app, go to Profile → Delete account. No app? Use the delete your data page.

How do I get the app?

Alate is in early access on Android (Google Play testing) and iPhone (TestFlight). Write to us for an invite.
