Common questions

  • How does a fit check work? Answer ten quick questions about your body, then give Alate a clothing product link — paste it, share it from the store's app or website, or copy it and open the fit-check panel. Alate returns a size to try and specific fit notes tailored to you.
  • Can I enter my exact measurements? Entering your own numbers, scanning a tailor's measurement sheet and sharing your measurements are Advanced features. Ask for them in Profile → Measurements, under "Enter your measurements"; once you're approved, they unlock on every device signed in to your account.
  • A brand or link didn't work. Some stores can't be read yet. If the brand isn't supported yet, tap "Notify me when added" on the result and we'll tell you when it is. A few stores have asked not to be read, or block automated reading; for those, the result links you straight to the store instead.
  • My size is sold out. Tap "Notify" on the fit card and the app tells you when that size is back — and, if you're signed in, emails you too.
  • Do I need an account? No. Fit checks work with no sign-in. Google sign-in is optional; if you use it, your profile, fit history and the pieces you've marked as bought sync across your devices.
  • Where is my data? Your body profile and fit history live on your device. If you sign in, a copy is kept in your account so your other devices can pull it. Details in the privacy policy.
  • How do I delete my account? In the app, go to Profile → Delete account. No app? Use the delete your data page.
